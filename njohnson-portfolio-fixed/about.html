A little about me

Curious about how games work.

I've spent years making games, prototypes, experiments, and small projects to understand what makes an idea fun once a player actually gets their hands on it.

My work has covered gameplay programming, systems design, level design, AI, prototyping, horror, first-person shooters, multiplayer experiments, and educational simulations.

I enjoy the entire process: getting something playable quickly, finding the problems through playtesting, and turning those discoveries into better design.
