A world that should be dead.

Hollowgrave is a first-person dungeon crawler built around a dying world, strange creatures, fast movement, and a heavy atmosphere of decay. It is the current project where several ideas I've explored across earlier prototypes are coming together.

The game mixes retro first-person presentation with volumetric lighting and fog, while keeping movement quick and responsive.

A strange companion.

One of the game's central visual ideas is a small floating eye held by the player. Most of the time it watches the player, but when something important is nearby, its attention shifts toward it.

Design goal: use a character's behavior as a subtle information system rather than relying entirely on UI.
Eye companion concept / gameplay

Movement, combat, and discovery.

The design is built around fast first-person movement, weapons, enemy encounters, environmental discovery, and the feeling that something is always waiting deeper in the dungeon.

This project is also an opportunity to experiment with how atmosphere and gameplay systems can reinforce each other instead of feeling like separate layers.

Building the idea.

Hollowgrave is being developed iteratively. Prototypes, visual experiments, enemy ideas, and procedural dungeon work are being tested as separate pieces before they are committed to the larger game.

This page is intentionally structured as a living case study. As development progresses, screenshots, videos, design notes, and technical breakdowns can be added here.
